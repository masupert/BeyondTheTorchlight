## Establish a Safe Playing Environment
The game can create uncomfortable situations and may contain episodes of horror due to the nature of the play at the table. Before the start of the game, there should be a discussion with everyone playing to establish what should be expected and what is tolerated at the table. 

Players and characters should not be shamed in any way for how they look, play, speak, dress or participate in the game. This is expected of every player at the table. 

The number one rule for anyone playing the game is to make sure that the other players the table are having fund first. 

## Fleshing out the Town
### Town Upkeep
- Town Cost is 500c per month to be paid by all residents in total. Up to four players. 
	- Cost increases by 200c for each additional player or at the following character tiers.

### Treasure vs. Coin
When adapting adventures from other games, magic items should generally be treated as Treasure. These are known as Artifacts. Other large monetary values should be converted into “Treasure”. Treasure does not have a specific monetary value to coin but is incredibly useful for improvements for the town or obtaining other finished goods from the civilized world.

# At the Table
## Guidance on Rolling Dice
- Player’s choices at the table and interacting with each other are more important than rolling dice to succeed.
- Try to keep rolling only for situations when the stakes matter, the outcome is uncertain, or the players may be hurt.
- Apply Enhancements and Impairments to provide or restrict information without rolling. 
- Failed rolls do not need to mean that the player wasn’t successful in their attempt. It just means that there was a consequence of their attempt. 
- There are no critical successes or failures, but you can have a degree of success or failure if you choose. 
## Make Rulings
- The rules in the game are fairly light, but even with the existing rules, the GM has the leeway to make rulings based upon the specific situation if it furthers the story or fun at the table. Rulings should be consistently applied to the game going forward or be declared that it is an exception for this specific situation and explained why. 
- Ask the players for their input on a ruling if it changes any of the existing rules in the game. 
## Dealing with Player Deaths
- Let players know that if they fail they may die with a skill check. 
- Player deaths don’t always have to be meaningful, but make sure you acknowledge the death at the game table. 
	- Encourage players to have a moment to react to a death when it happens.
